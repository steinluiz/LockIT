## 🪤 Traps

### ✨ Changes & Improvements
- **Traps** — Protect a lock with a trap. Open the lock menu and put a trap in the **fourth slot**. The trap goes off on a robber each time they click a **wrong pin** while picking the lock.
- **Three traps:**
  - **Ink Trap** — Blinds the robber, makes them glow and throws them out of the lockpicking menu.
  - **Sound Trap** — Sounds a loud alarm and tells nearby players where it went off. Throws the robber out of the lockpicking menu.
  - **Spike Trap** — Damages the robber. They can keep picking.
- **A trap needs a lock** — A trap only stays in a block that has a lock with a code. Otherwise it is given back when you close the menu.
- **Removing a lock gives the trap back** — Take the lock out and close the menu: the trap comes back to you too.
- **Trap durability** — Each trap can go off a limited number of times, shown as **Uses left** in its tooltip. It breaks when none are left.
- **New lock menu layout** — The lock now goes in the **second slot** and the trap in the **fourth**. The menu has a new texture.
- **New `traps.yml`** — For each trap:
  - `durability` — How many times it can go off (`0` = never breaks). Defaults: Ink `3`, Sound `5`, Spike `10`.
  - `kick` — Throw the robber out of the lockpicking menu when it goes off. Default `true` for Ink and Sound, `false` for Spike.
  - Ink `blindness-seconds` and `glowing-seconds`, Sound `alert-radius`, Spike `damage`.
- **Crafting** — Recipes for all three traps in `craftings.yml`, and their names and `custom_model_data` (`110`, `111`, `112`) in `items.yml`.
- **New command** — `/lockit trap <ink|sound|spike>`.
- **Resource pack** — Textures for the three traps and the new lock menu, and a 3D key model when holding a key. If your `config.yml` still uses the pack from an older LockIT version, it is switched to the new one automatically.

### 🛠️ Fixes & Tweaks
- Swapping the lock in the lock menu for a different lock now installs the new one. Before, the new lock was lost and the old one was duplicated.
- New options added by an update now work right away. Before, some only applied after a second restart (new items showed up as stone named "Item Error").
- Locks, keys and lockpicks from older versions keep working, so nothing needs migrating.
